# ⚡ EV Market Intelligence Dashboard
### An Interactive Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Solution for the Electric Vehicle Industry

---

## 📌 1. Project Overview

The **EV Market Intelligence Dashboard** is an interactive, data-driven business intelligence solution designed to analyze and visualize key trends in the Electric Vehicle (EV) industry.

The dashboard helps users understand the evolving EV market through meaningful insights into vehicle categories, manufacturers, battery technologies, driving ranges, pricing, geographical distribution, annual sales, and customer ratings.

Built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the dashboard provides an intuitive interface where users can upload their own datasets in CSV or Excel format and instantly generate interactive visualizations and key performance indicators (KPIs).

The solution aims to support data-driven decision-making for automotive businesses, market researchers, EV manufacturers, business strategists, and sustainability professionals.

---

## 🎯 2. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- **Analyze EV Market Trends:** Understand the distribution and performance of electric vehicles across different categories and manufacturers.
- **Evaluate Battery Technologies:** Compare battery types, battery capacities, and their impact on vehicle driving range.
- **Monitor Sales Performance:** Analyze annual EV sales and identify leading manufacturers.
- **Understand Customer Preferences:** Evaluate customer ratings across different EV categories.
- **Explore Geographical Distribution:** Identify major EV manufacturing countries and regional market patterns.
- **Enable Data-Driven Decisions:** Convert raw EV datasets into meaningful visual insights that support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🚀 3. Key Features

### A. Interactive Dashboard
- Modern, responsive dashboard with a professional dark-themed interface.
- Real-time chart updates based on selected filters.
- Interactive visualizations powered by Plotly.js.
- User-friendly interface for exploring EV market data.

### B. Dataset Upload Functionality
- Upload datasets directly from your computer.
- Supports CSV (.csv), Excel (.xlsx), and Excel (.xls) formats.
- Automatically processes uploaded data in the browser.
- Refreshes dashboard KPIs and charts based on the uploaded dataset.

### C. Dynamic Filtering System
Users can filter the dashboard based on:

- Market Region
- Vehicle Category
- Model Year
- Battery Type

All charts and KPIs automatically update according to the selected filters.

### D. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard displays five important performance indicators:

1. **Total Vehicle Records:** Number of vehicle records available in the selected dataset.
2. **Average Battery Capacity:** Average battery capacity measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
3. **Average Driving Range:** Average distance covered by an EV on a single charge, measured in kilometres.
4. **Total Annual Sales:** Combined annual sales units represented in the selected dataset.
5. **Average Customer Rating:** Average customer satisfaction rating on a scale of 5.
